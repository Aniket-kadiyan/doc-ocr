Manual verification
-------------------
1. Run Auto Balloon on a drawing that creates at least two review candidates.
2. Select one review candidate.
3. Confirm its box is strongly highlighted and other balloons/review boxes dim.
4. Press Cancel in the review popup.
5. Confirm the selected review box remains highlighted.
6. Confirm the highlight clears when the candidate is accepted or ignored.
7. Confirm it also clears when another value/review item or blank drawing area
   is clicked.
8. Click Hide Balloons. Confirm accepted value rectangles are covered in white
   while review candidates, references and GD&T overlays remain available.
9. Click Show Balloons. Confirm the original drawing and balloons return.
