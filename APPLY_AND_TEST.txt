What this changes
-----------------
- Preserves and normalizes depth, counterbore, countersink, and diameter glyphs.
- Accepts complete hole callouts such as:
  - Ø2.50 ↧13
  - M3x0.5 - 6H ↧3
  - 2 x Ø2.50 THRU ALL
  - Ø4 H7 ↧10
  - Ø6.50 ⌴Ø11 ↧4.40
  - Ø3.50 ⌵Ø7 X 90°
- Accepts and structurally parses ISO fits such as Ø4 H7, Ø18h6, and 20 JS9.
- Keeps lowercase shaft-fit letters lowercase because case is meaningful.
- Repairs a depth glyph read as T only when the repaired text is a complete
  hole callout; part numbers and ordinary text are left unchanged.
- Records complete hole/depth structures in engineering_parse so they become
  eligible instead of being reduced to a leading number or sent to review.
- Enables Depth and Counterbore classification for these complete values.
