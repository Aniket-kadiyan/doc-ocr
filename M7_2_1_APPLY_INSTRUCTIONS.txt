What this milestone fixes
-------------------------
- Detects the specific native-PDF geometry where the lower zero of a stacked
  tolerance is accidentally appended to the nominal (for example, 21 plus
  upper +1 and lower 0 being grouped as 210 plus +1).
- Reconstructs that value as 21 +1/0 without changing any source digit.
- After candidate-local degree geometry is confirmed, publishes the result as
  21°+1°/0°.
- Leaves unresolved or multiply matched structures visible in Review.
- Prevents degree enrichment from automatically accepting a structurally
  ambiguous or conflicting native assembly.
- Keeps an ordinary 210 whose digits share one baseline unchanged.
- Preserves every original native run in assembly_children for audit.
