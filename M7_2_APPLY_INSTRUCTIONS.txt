What this milestone changes
---------------------------
- Joins adjacent native PDF fragments before page filtering. A nominal such as
  22.9 and its separate ±1 fragment are evaluated as one value instead of
  allowing ±1 to be rejected as a frame label.
- Keeps native PDF characters and digits authoritative. The new enrichment
  pass does not call OCR and does not substitute OCR text.
- Restores degree marks only from a small crop around a plausible native angle.
- Resolves horizontal and vertical feature-control frames from local frame
  geometry while retaining native tolerance and datum text.
- Preserves explicit surface-finish text such as Rz12.5 even when nearby
  section/detail wording would otherwise exclude it.
- Sends FCF-like native text with unresolved frame geometry to Review.
- Keeps every absorbed native fragment in assembly_children for audit/review.
